Good questions to ask

Before you make the move

Can foreigners retire in Paraguay?

Foreign nationals may be eligible for Paraguayan residence under applicable immigration rules. Requirements and procedures can change; verify them with Paraguay’s official migration authorities or a qualified local professional before making plans.

Is healthcare available outside major cities?

Access to specialists and facilities differs by region. Research hospitals, emergency transportation, insurance, and prescription availability for each prospective location.

Should I rent or buy first?

Renting first can help you experience seasonal weather, transportation, utilities, neighborhood life, and healthcare access before committing to a purchase.

Do I need to speak Spanish or Guaraní?

Spanish is very useful for daily life and administration; Guaraní is also widely spoken. Learning some of both can make everyday interactions easier.
